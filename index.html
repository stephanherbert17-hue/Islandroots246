The Island Rootz Story

Rooted in Barbados. Built for the world.

Island Rootz is a Barbadian musical entertainment company creating high-energy experiences for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, festivals, cultural celebrations and private functions.

Our sound and movement are inspired by the spirit of Barbados while our performance formats can be shaped for audiences anywhere in the world.
