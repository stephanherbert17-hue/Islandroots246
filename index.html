The Rootz

Not just a band. A full experience.

Founded and directed by Stephan Herbert, Island Rootz combines music, Barbadian culture, choreography and visual energy.

From birthdays and weddings to corporate events, festivals, concerts and international engagements, every production can be tailored to the occasion.
