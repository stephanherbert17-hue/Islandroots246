About IslandRootz

More than a band.

IslandRootz is a Barbadian musical entertainment company built around live performance, creativity, culture and professional event experiences.

Our roots include Barbadian cultural performance and Tuk, but IslandRootz is not limited to one musical style. We bring together musicians, performers and dancers to create entertainment experiences designed around each event.

Client Information

Booking Terms.

01 · Enquiry

A submitted enquiry does not automatically confirm the requested date or performance.

02 · Quotation

Pricing is based on the agreed entertainment package, performers, duration, location, logistics and production requirements.

03 · Confirmation

A booking becomes confirmed only after the final package, price, timing and requirements have been agreed in writing.

04 · Event Day

The client is responsible for providing the agreed venue access, performance area and relevant site requirements.

05 · Changes

Changes to timing, venue, duration, performer numbers or package requirements may result in an adjusted quotation.

06 · Final Agreement

The final written booking agreement takes precedence over general information published on this website.
