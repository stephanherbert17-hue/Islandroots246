Who We Are

Rooted in Barbados. Made to move people.

IslandRootz is a Barbadian musical entertainment company creating live experiences for celebrations, festivals, corporate events, weddings and private functions.

Our roots are grounded in Barbadian cultural performance, including Tuk, while our entertainment can move from a full concert-band experience to dancers, cultural elements or a custom combination designed around your event.
