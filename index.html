The Island Rootz Experience

More than music. It's a feeling.

Island Rootz brings together the sound, movement and spirit of Barbados to create entertainment experiences people remember.


Founded and directed by Stephan Herbert, Island Rootz works with clients to build performances around birthdays, weddings, corporate events, concerts, cultural celebrations, festivals and custom occasions.
